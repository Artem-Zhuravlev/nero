Files: montserrat-<latin|cyrillic>-<400|500|600|700|800>.woff2 (unicode-range split in stylesheet.css).

- Golos Text (c) The Golos Text Project Authors, https://github.com/googlefonts/golos-text
- Nunito Sans (c) The Nunito Sans Project Authors, https://github.com/Fonthausen/NunitoSans
Files: <key>-<latin|cyrillic>-<weight>.woff2 with key = inter, manrope, onest, golos-text, nunito-sans.

Commercial families (Gilroy, Proxima Nova, Geometria) are NOT bundled. They become selectable in the theme settings once licensed
files named gilroy-/proxima-nova-/geometria-<latin|cyrillic>-<weight>.woff2 (at least 400 and 700 in both scripts) are placed here.
